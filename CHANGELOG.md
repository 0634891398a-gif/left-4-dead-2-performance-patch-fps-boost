# CHANGELOG.md

## [v2.4.1] - 2023-10-15
### Improvements
- Optimized server-side hit registration, reducing latency during high-traffic moments.
- Enhanced AI director responsiveness, leading to smoother pacing and fewer abrupt changes in gameplay dynamics.
- Tweaked rendering settings for better frame rates in dense environments like Dark Carnival and The Parish.

### Fixes
- Fixed an issue where players could experience stuttering during special infected spawn phases.
- Resolved a bug causing frame drops in the finale of No Mercy when multiple events triggered simultaneously.
- Corrected audio synchronization issues with certain weapons and special infected sounds.

---

## [v2.3.0] - 2023-08-05
### Improvements
- Introduced a new graphical option for dynamic shadows, enhancing visual clarity during gameplay.
- Adjusted weapon recoil behavior to provide a more consistent experience across different settings.
- Updated the matchmaking algorithm to reduce wait times for lobby connections.

### Fixes
- Patched a crash that occurred when using the “Scavenge” game mode with a full server.
- Fixed rendering issues with the environment in the Dead Air campaign during foggy weather conditions.
- Resolved discrepancies in survivor spawning locations in the Swamp Fever campaign.

---

## [v2.1.0] - 2023-05-12
### Improvements
- Updated AI pathfinding for Common Infected, resulting in more intelligent movement around obstacles.
- Streamlined the load times for levels with heavy asset usage, specifically in the Dead Center campaign.
- Improved texture streaming performance leading to reduced pop-in for distant objects.

### Fixes
- Fixed a persistent glitch causing players to become stuck in the tutorial levels.
- Resolved the visual glitch affecting the Molotov cocktail's flame effects when used at long ranges.
- Corrected issues where players would intermittently not receive credits after completing a campaign on expert mode.

---

## [v2.0.0] - 2023-01-29
### New Features
- Introduced a new "Arcade Mode" for more casual play with modified rules and unique achievement rewards.
- Added support for mouse acceleration adjustment in-game settings for improved aiming precision.

### Improvements
- Enhanced overall performance on lower-end hardware, allowing more players to enjoy the game with stable frame rates.
- Revised the weather system to add more variety in rain and fog effects across different campaigns.
